Game: Pong
Authors: Jayden Pope & Jared Melchers

Instructions:
1. Load game onto UCFK using "make program" command
2. Align UCFK so the IR transmitters and receivers are aligned
3. One person press the button above the screen to start the game
4. Use the navswitch to move your platform (closest to you) left and right
5. Hit the ball with your platform
6. You win if your opponet misses the ball (winner indicated by the blue LED)
7. To restart press the button again
